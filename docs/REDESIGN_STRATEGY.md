## 1. Design Strategy & Brand Positioning

The redesign strategy transforms the structural 12-section reference layout into a bespoke, high-end digital practice for Dr. Maya Reynolds. 

### Brand Personality & Aesthetic Values
- **Tone:** Warm, collaborative, grounded, sophisticated, clinical, and human.
- **Visual Mood:** Quiet, private, uncluttered, filled with natural light, and therapeutic.
- **Target Audience Alignment:** Thoughtful, high-achieving adults, entrepreneurs, creatives, and professionals in California who appreciate clarity, depth, and refinement.
- **Aesthetic Guidance:** 
  - Avoid generic wellness clichés, bright pastel gradients, decorative clutter, or cold clinical stock photography.
  - Emphasize spacious margins, crisp serif typography, serene organic color tones, and tactile card surfaces.

## 3. Typography System

- **Display & Headings Font:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Font)
  - *Rationale:* Warm serif with high editorial elegance and clinical authority.
- **Body & UI Font:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Google Font)
  - *Rationale:* Exceptionally clean, humanistic sans-serif for maximum readability on all screens.
- **Typography Scale:**
  - **H1 (Hero):** `40px` (Mobile) / `56px` (Desktop) | Semi-Bold (`font-semibold`), leading `1.15`
  - **H2 (Section Headings):** `28px` (Mobile) / `40px` (Desktop) | Bold (`font-bold`), leading `1.2`
  - **H3 (Subheadings):** `22px` (Mobile) / `28px` (Desktop) | Medium (`font-medium`)
  - **H4 (Card Titles):** `18px` / `20px` | Bold (`font-bold`)
  - **Body Copy:** `16px` (Mobile) / `18px` (Desktop) | Normal (`font-normal`), leading `1.65`
  - **Eyebrow / Badges:** `12px` / `13px` | Uppercase, bold, letter-spacing `0.15em` (`tracking-widest`)

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. **Who do you work with in your practice?**  
   *Answer:* I specialize in working with adults who are experiencing anxiety, panic, trauma, professional burnout, perfectionism, or chronic stress. Many of my clients are high-achieving adults, entrepreneurs, creatives, and professionals who feel exhausted or emotionally on edge despite being functional on the outside.
2. **What therapy approaches and modalities do you use?**  
   *Answer:* I integrate evidence-based methods tailored to your needs, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing), mindfulness-based practices, and body-oriented techniques to address both emotional and physiological symptoms.
3. **How do you approach trauma therapy?**  
   *Answer:* Trauma work in my practice is carefully paced with an explicit focus on safety and stabilization first. I work with adults dealing with single-incident trauma as well as complex, long-standing childhood or relational patterns, helping you feel more regulated in daily life.
4. **Do you offer in-person therapy sessions?**  
   *Answer:* Yes, I provide in-person therapy from my quiet, private office located at 123th Street 45 W in Santa Monica, California.
5. **Do you offer telehealth or online therapy?**  
   *Answer:* Yes, I offer secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth video sessions for adult clients located anywhere within the state of California.
6. **What is your Santa Monica office environment like?**  
   *Answer:* My office is a quiet, private space designed to feel calm, grounding, and uncluttered, with natural light and comfortable seating to help you feel at ease when you arrive.
